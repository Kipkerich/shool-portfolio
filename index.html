Courses Offered

Health Support Services

This program is designed to equip you with the vital knowledge and practical skills required for immediate, impactful contributions to patient care and healthcare efficiency. Through hands-on training and mentorship, you will master essential supportive functions, including infection control, hospital procedures, and effective patient interaction. By course completion, you will be prepared for entry-level roles as a healthcare assistant, patient attendant, or clinical support specialist, ready to thrive in the dynamic healthcare environment.

6 Months Fees Structure

Perioperative Theatre Technology

Learn the essential skills and knowledge required to work in the operating theatre environment. This program covers pre-operative, intra-operative, and post-operative care procedures. You will gain hands-on experience in sterile techniques, surgical instrument handling, and patient monitoring. Prepare for a career in perioperative care with this comprehensive training program.

3 Months Fees Structure

Phlebotomy Technician

Master the art of blood collection and specimen handling in this specialized phlebotomy training program. Learn proper venipuncture techniques, patient communication, and safety protocols. This course provides hands-on experience in drawing blood from various patients and processing specimens accurately. Upon completion, you will be ready to work in hospitals, clinics, laboratories, and other healthcare facilities.

6 Weeks Fees Structure

First Aid

Gain essential life-saving skills in first aid and cardiopulmonary resuscitation (CPR) with this comprehensive training program. Learn to respond effectively to medical emergencies, including choking, bleeding, fractures, and cardiac arrest. This course combines theoretical knowledge with practical hands-on training to prepare you for real-life emergency situations. Upon completion, you will be certified in first aid and CPR techniques.

5 Days Fees Structure
