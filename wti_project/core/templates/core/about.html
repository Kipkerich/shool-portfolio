Who We Are

About Wama Training Institute

A TVETA accredited medical institution dedicated to producing highly skilled healthcare professionals.

Advancing Medical Knowledge

Wama Training Institute (WTI) is located in Ongata Rongai, Kenya. We offer premium training inside modern skills laboratories and direct clinical attachments within active, fully-equipped medical centers.

Our Vision, Mission & Values

Our Mission

To excel the community in medical knowledge and provide the best care in and outside hospital, locally and internationally, to bridge the gap in the healthcare system.

Our Vision

To be a highly ranked health college that provides dynamic, comprehensive, and globally competitive healthcare courses.

Our Values

W.A.M.A.P.E.V: Welfare, Accountability, Mindfulness, Accessibility, Professionalism, Excellence, and Value Addition & Improvement.
